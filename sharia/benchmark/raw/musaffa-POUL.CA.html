Is Cairo Poultry Company SAE Stock Halal?

Shariah Compliance Analysis of POUL.CA Stock

Egypt Consumer Staples Small Cap

No. As of November 2026, Cairo Poultry Company SAE (POUL.CA) is classified as NOT HALAL by Musaffa under its AAOIFI-based Shariah screening methodology. Cairo Poultry Company SAE does not pass Musaffa's business and financial screening criteria.

Cairo Poultry Co. engages in the management of poultry business. The firm focuses on the production, breeding and fattening chicks; production of animal feed, and hatching of eggs, as well as participating in similar projects inside and outside Egypt. The firm also focuses on other related industries, such as starch and glucose, logistics and feed ingredients trading. The firm has investment in its subsidiaries, which include New Cairo Poultry Co., Cairo for Poultry Slaughterhouse and Wadi Al Natroun for Mothers of Poultry, among others.
