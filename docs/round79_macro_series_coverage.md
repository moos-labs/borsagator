# Round 79: macro series coverage (what is collected, what is missing)

Scope: KEEP 5 YEARS (2021-11-27 to 2026-11-27). Every value below carries the page it was read from; nothing is from memory. Format: value, observed_date, published_date, source_url, source_name, readable as-is by benchmarks/regime_macro_tilt_shadow.py (load_rows: 30 rows, 0 rejected; load_decisions: 30 usable, 0 rejected).

## Collected
- evaluation/macro_series/cbe_overnight_deposit_rate.csv: CBE overnight deposit rate after each MPC decision, 30 decisions from 2023-03-30 to 2026-10-29. observed_date = published_date = the decision date (the release time is unsettled, so the regime uses it from the next session). Each source is the CBE MPC press release page (English; Arabic where the English page is not served to the fetcher: 2023-06-22, 2024-09-05, 2025-02-20; PDF for 2023-09-21 and 2023-11-02). Levels were read from each page's text.
- evaluation/macro_series/cbe_decisions.json: the change in basis points per decision, the scaffold's decision format. Four changes are as stated in the release (2023-03-30 +200, 2023-08-03 +100, 2024-02-01 +200, 2024-03-06 +600); the others are the difference of consecutive sourced levels (the pages state the level or "unchanged"; for 2025-04-17 the release states a 225 bp cut and the level difference agrees).
- evaluation/macro_series/regime_counts_descriptive.json: the scaffold's counts-only run on the real ledger with these decisions. Result: all 309 captures have a regime and ALL are "tightening" (the last decision before every capture is the 29 Oct 2026 hike), 13 cutoff dates per horizon. The current ledger therefore has no regime variation; the regime tilt cannot be compared across regimes on it.

- cpi_annual_urban_headline.csv: annual urban headline CPI inflation (percent), 34 months from 2023-03 to 2026-10. Each row is read from the CBE "CPI Press Release <month>" page (CAPMAS data), the value for the reference month named in that page. observed_date = reference month (YYYY-MM), published_date = the CBE page date taken from the page URL (not the CAPMAS release time, which can be earlier).

## Round 81 additions and audit
- Added 2023-02-02 (hold, deposit 16.25): the CBE Annual Report 2022/2023 states the MPC "decided in its fifth meeting dated February 2nd, 2023 to keep" the deposit, lending and main-operation rates unchanged at 16.25, 17.25 and 16.75 percent. This was a real gap: without it the regime read "tightening" for 2023-02-03 to 2023-03-30; it is now "hold". Only this row changes a regime classification.
- Added 2022-08-18 (hold, deposit 11.25): the same report states "the MPC decided in its two meetings dated August 18 and September 22, 2022 to keep" the rates unchanged at 11.25, 12.25 and 11.75 percent. Completeness only: it sits between two holds.
- Source for both is the annual-report PDF (https://www.cbe.org.eg/-/media/project/cbe/listing/research/annual-report/annual-report-2022-2023_-en.pdf), not a per-meeting press-release page: searches of the CBE site did not return the 18 Aug 2022 or 2 Feb 2023 release pages and guessed URLs did not resolve. Levels are as stated in the report; change_bps 0 follows from "kept unchanged".
- 2022-11-03: NOT added. No CBE source read states a decision or meeting on that date. The same report numbers the FY 2022/23 meetings: 18 Aug and 22 Sept 2022 (first two, the second named "second meeting on September 22nd"), the special 27 Oct 2022 meeting (named in the main-actions list), "fourth meeting held on December 22nd, 2022", "fifth meeting dated February 2nd, 2023". Counting 18 Aug, 22 Sep, 27 Oct, 22 Dec and 2 Feb gives five, which leaves no room for a 3 Nov meeting (an inference from the ordinals; no page says "no meeting on 3 Nov"). The only CBE page dated 2022-11-03 found is a sustainability-regulations release, not an MPC decision.
- Pre-2023-03-30 count correction: the earlier line "8 decisions" listed the 2021-12-16 to 2022-12-22 calendar. Meetings confirmed from the report in the 5-year scope before 2023-03-30: 2021-12-16 and 2022-02-03 (no dates stated, excluded), 2022-03-21, 05-19, 06-23, 08-18, 09-22, 10-27, 12-22 and 2023-02-02 = 10 confirmed, 8 included, 2 excluded. The "11 decisions" figure in the round 81 brief matches only if the unconfirmed 2022-11-03 is counted.
- Test pin: test_collected_series in scripts/test_regime_macro_tilt_shadow.py pins all 38 (date, bps) rows, Feb 2023 = hold, after 2023-03-30 = tightening, and fails if either new row is dropped.
