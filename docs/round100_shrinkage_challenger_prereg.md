## Baseline, defined from scratch (author's choice)
For name i, horizon h in {day, week, month}, cutoff C with base date B (the cutoff close, as in the capture):
- Sessions per horizon: S_h = 1, 5, 21 (the repo's SESSIONS table, the same one `half_pct` uses).
- Realised moves: for every session t in the name's OHLCV close series, m(t) = 100 x (close(t + S_h) / close(t) - 1). Overlapping windows, simple percent move, same unit as `real` in the rows.
- Window: the 250 most recent sessions ending on or before B whose END close is dated on or before B (so every m(t) used had matured at B; nothing after B is read). Fewer than 125 usable moves: the (name, cutoff, horizon) pair is excluded from the challenger with reason "short history" and listed individually.
- Quantiles: q_lo = 10th percentile and q_hi = 90th percentile of those moves, linear interpolation between order statistics (index (n-1) x p, standard definition). 10/90 is the author's choice to match the 0.75-0.85 coverage target of the loop bar; it is a fixed constant and is never tuned.
- Dividend-gap rows and immature rows follow the existing row builder (rows with a dividend gap or not matured are not rows).

## Challenger (exact)
Frozen v2 edges: L_v2 = -half, U_v2 = +half, half = the stored v2 half-width in percent (computed by the existing `half_pct`, never refit).
Challenger edges per horizon h: L = a_h x L_v2 + (1 - a_h) x q_lo, U = b_h x U_v2 + (1 - b_h) x q_hi, with ONE shared weight pair (a, b) in [0, 1] for all three horizons (AMENDED, see Amendment 1 below; the first draft had two weights per horizon). If the resulting L >= U the row is scored with the v2 interval and listed with reason "crossed edges".
The weight pair is frozen after the fit and shared by all names, cutoffs and horizons.

## Fit (cells 1-47 only)
- Objective: mean interval score (the repo's `winkler_is`, the same score RangeSkill uses) of the challenger over the POOLED covered training rows of all three horizons, minimised over the grid a, b in {0, 0.05, ..., 1.0} (21 x 21, one shared pair). Ties go to the larger weight (nearer v2). Grid and tie rule are the author's, fixed now.
- Covered rows: rows of any horizon from cells 1-47 that have a realised close, that pass the "short history" rule, and whose realised close is dated BEFORE 2025-08-15 (freeze-date rule, applied here by the author because the same leak was found in #961: cells 44-47 realise about 2025-10-29/30, after the earliest scored cutoff 2025-08-15). Rows failing a rule are excluded from the fit, do not count toward the threshold, and are listed individually with cutoff, name, horizon and reason ("no realised close", "close after freeze date", "short history").
- Fewer than 100 covered rows pooled over the three horizons: no weights are fit, the challenger is skipped, and the run reports counts only. The pooled covered-row count and the per-horizon covered counts are printed first.
- Cells 48-90 and 91+ never change a weight.

## Windows
- Fit: cells 1-47. Descriptive: cells 48-90, counts and paired RangeSkill delta (challenger minus frozen v2, same rows), coverage, cutoff-clustered bootstrap, no verdict word. Score: untouched cells 91+ only, never loaded before this document is merged. No verdict-cell origin is read for smoke tests (use pre-2025-10-20 contexts).

## Intervals and loop bar
95% percentile bootstrap resampling CUTOFF DATES with replacement, 5000 reps, seed 20261125, default reps only (any other `--reps` refused before loading inputs). A horizon is KEPT only when, on untouched cells, (1) the challenger-minus-v2 RangeSkill cutoff-clustered CI lower bound > 0, (2) the cutoff-clustered coverage CI overlaps [0.75, 0.85] with width <= 0.15, and (3) at least 10 cutoff clusters back the horizon (a cluster counts only when every intended row of that cutoff has a realised close; a partly mature cluster does not count, and the single KEEP decision uses only clusters fully mature at the decision date). Fewer than 10 clusters: COUNTS ONLY, no coverage, skill or CI printed or written. Rows with no realised close are excluded from scoring entirely and listed individually. A KEEP is not an adoption. Cells 91+ hold few clusters today, so no KEEP is reachable until the forward weekly schedule (#953) accrues clusters.

## Known limits (author)
- Per-name quantiles from 250 overlapping sessions are noisy for the month horizon (about 12 independent windows); the 125-move rule only guarantees overlapping count.
- The baseline uses the name's own recent unconditional moves, so it is a width-and-centre object. Adjacency to the closed width family is accepted by Moos; the argument for it is only that six weights replace re-estimation of volatility.
- The panel is about 6 to 8 large-cap names; descriptive read has 8 cutoff dates.
- Weights are shared across names. The fit cannot use any cell whose realised close post-dates 2025-08-15.

- KEEP rule, per horizon, all four required: (1) the lower bound of the challenger-minus-v2 RangeSkill CI is > 0; (2) the coverage CI overlaps [0.75, 0.85]; (3) the coverage CI width (upper minus lower) is <= 0.15; (4) at least 10 fully mature cutoff clusters back the horizon.

A partly mature cluster does not count, and the single KEEP decision uses only clusters fully mature at the decision date. Rows with no realised close are excluded from scoring entirely and listed individually with cutoff, name, horizon and reason "no realised close".
- Fewer than 10 fully mature clusters: COUNTS ONLY (counts and reasons, no coverage, skill or CI printed or written).
- A KEEP is not an adoption. The decision is evaluated once, at the decision date; later data do not reopen it.

## Amendment 4 (mature-cluster counting implemented in the scorer)
Moos decision, Option 1 (Slack 1795855261.402599, relayed, read by the author via the Slack thread read): the scorer now computes the cluster-counting rule instead of leaving it to hand counting. `benchmarks/score_shrinkage_challenger.py` `mature_clusters(caps, horizon_rows)`, same rule as the #969 week scorer, applied per horizon. Intended rows of a cutoff = one per in-range capture of that cutoff (its ticker), for the horizon being counted. No eligibility filter applies: a captured ticker whose row for that horizon is dropped (capture rejection, v2_sigma missing, short history, no realised close) stays intended, so its cluster is not mature. A cutoff counts only when every intended ticker has a usable row with a realised close. Duplicate captures collapse and rows from other cutoffs never count. The registered minimum is 10 (`MIN_CLUSTERS`). This clarifies the parenthetical "(all names that cutoff would score for that horizon)" in the cluster-counting rule above: it means the captured tickers of that cutoff, with no eligibility filter. Describe mode prints "fully mature cutoff clusters=N of M" per horizon before the statistics and writes `mature_clusters` to the JSON. No change to the frozen pair, the weights, the rules above or any existing number. On cells 48-90 every horizon shows 8 of 8 cutoff clusters mature, below the minimum of 10; the descriptive statistics already in this document are descriptive only.
